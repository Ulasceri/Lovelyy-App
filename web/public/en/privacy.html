Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Lovelyy is a mobile app where two partners raise a shared cat and keep shared memories. This policy explains what data we process, why, and your rights. Contact: TODO-iletisim@ornek.com

1. Data we process

DataPurpose
Account: email address, password (stored only, hashed, by Firebase Authentication), and for Google sign-in your Google account name and profile photo linkSign-in and account recognition
Profile: display name, pairing code, the ID of your couplePairing with your partner
Couple data: relationship start date, shared cat state and care streak, heartbeat and "thinking of you" signals, mood entries (and short notes you add), daily question and your answers, notes, calendar and special days, bucket list, decision wheels and results, time-capsule letters, live and saved drawingsApp features; visible only to you and your paired partner
Approximate location (optional): city name and rounded coordinatesThe "Distance" feature
Usage and diagnostics: anonymous usage events (e.g. "cat fed", which screen opened), crash reports, performance measurements, device model and Android versionImproving the app and fixing bugs

We do not collect the advertising ID, the app shows no ads, and we do not sell your data.

2. Location

The location permission covers only approximate (city-level) location. You grant it and can stop sharing at any time on the Distance screen; stopping deletes your shared location. Coordinates are rounded to about 10 km (or 1 km if you choose) before leaving your phone. Your partner sees only your city and the distance between you. Location is not tracked in the background.

3. Who can see your data

  • Couple data is visible only to you and your paired partner (enforced by Firestore security rules).
  • Your partner sees your name and profile photo link.
  • Our processor is Google LLC (Firebase: Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring). Data may be stored in Europe (eur3) and other Google regions under Google's standard contractual clauses.

4. Retention

Account and couple data are kept until you delete them. If you unpair, shared data is permanently deleted for both partners. If you delete your account, your profile and Firebase account are deleted (if paired, the pairing is removed first and shared data is deleted). Crash and usage data are retained for a limited period by Google (typically up to 14 months) and are not linked to your identity.

5. Deleting your account and data

In the app: Us → Account → Delete account. If you cannot access the app, send a request on the account deletion page.

6. Your rights (GDPR / KVKK)

You may access, correct, delete, object to processing of, and port your data. You can change your name and delete your account in the app; email us for other requests. You may complain to your local data protection authority (in Turkey, the KVKK Board). Our legal bases are providing the service (contract), our legitimate interest in diagnostics and analytics, and your consent for location.

7. Children

Lovelyy is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn of such an account we delete it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored on Google's infrastructure. No system is entirely risk-free, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

9. Changes

If we update this policy we change the date above and announce significant changes in the app.
