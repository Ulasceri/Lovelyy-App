Delete Your Account and Data

This page explains how to delete your Lovelyy account and data.

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lovelyy and go to the Us tab.
  2. Tap Account → Delete account.
  3. If asked, sign in again with your password or Google and confirm.

Option 2: Request by email

If you cannot access the app (for example you lost your phone), write from the email address registered to your account: applovelyy@gmail.com

Subject: Lovelyy account deletion request. State your account email. We complete requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

Send deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, photo link, pairing code) and sign-in account (email, password).
  • If you are paired, the pairing is removed first and shared data is permanently deleted for both partners: cat state, notes, letters, mood and answers, calendar, bucket list, wheels, drawings, shared location. Your partner's phone notices and closes the pairing; your partner keeps their own account.
  • Local data on your phone (app data, widget image) is removed when you uninstall the app.

What is kept

Anonymous usage, crash and performance data not linked to your identity may remain for Google's standard retention period (typically up to 14 months). We keep nothing else.

Deletion cannot be undone. See the Privacy Policy for details.
